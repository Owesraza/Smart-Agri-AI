## 🧮 Core CropPoint Proprietary Formulations Implemented in Paper

The following operational formulas are original engineering contributions synthesized in the paper:

1. **Recursive Exponential Moving Average (EMA) Momentum (Eq. 1, p. 3):**
   $$\text{EMA}_{N, t} = \alpha P_{t-1} + (1 - \alpha) \text{EMA}_{N, t-1}, \quad \alpha = \frac{2}{N + 1}, \quad N \in \{7, 14, 30\}$$

2. **Six-Factor Hybrid Smart Pricing Recommendation (Eq. 5 & 6, p. 3):**
   $$P_{\text{rec}} = 0.35 P_{\text{mandi}} + 0.25 P_{\text{ML}} + 0.20 P_{\text{EMA}} + 0.10 P_{\text{week}} + 0.05 P_{\text{weather}} + 0.05 P_{\text{hist}}$$
   $$P_{\text{final}} = P_{\text{rec}} \times \mu_Q \times \gamma_{\text{role}}$$
   *Where $\mu_Q \in \{1.02, 1.00, 0.95\}$ (Grades A/B/C) and $\gamma_{\text{role}} \in \{1.00 \text{ (Farmer, rounded to Rs. 10)}, 0.985 \text{ (Buyer)}\}$.*

3. **Geodesic Road Freight Calculation with Empirical Circuity Factor (Eq. 9 & 10, p. 4):**
   $$d_{\text{road}} = 1.25 \times 2 R \arcsin\left(\sqrt{\sin^2\left(\frac{\Delta\phi}{2}\right) + \cos\phi_1\cos\phi_2\sin^2\left(\frac{\Delta\lambda}{2}\right)}\right)$$
   $$C_f = d_{\text{road}} \times 0.50 \times Q$$

4. **Autonomous Net Farm Profit Accounting (Eq. 11, p. 4):**
   $$\text{NetProfit} = A_{\text{total}} - (C_f + C_{\text{packaging}} + A_{\text{fee}})$$
